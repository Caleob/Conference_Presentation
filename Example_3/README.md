# Example 3: Elastic Timeline

## What is this app?
This is an interactive timeline game where cards float gently at the bottom of the screen. You or your students can drag and drop the cards onto a timeline bar at the top, and the cards smoothly slide apart to make room. When you click "Check Timeline," the app lights up the cards to show which ones are in the right order.

## How to use it in a virtual K-12 classroom
* **Chronology Review During Screen Shares:** Share your screen during a live virtual lesson. Read the floating events aloud with the class, ask students in the chat or microphone what comes next, and drag the cards into place together.
* **Smart Color Feedback:** When you click "Check Timeline," cards placed in the correct chronological story turn bright green. Cards placed out of order glow yellow so students can see where the chain broke without feeling defeated.
* **Instant Custom Lessons:** Click the "Edit List" button and paste any list of events in order (events from the American Revolution, plot points from a novel, or the stages of the water cycle). The app shuffles them and builds a brand-new game instantly.
* **Celebration Confetti:** When the whole sequence is solved correctly, colorful confetti bursts across the screen to celebrate student success!

## What a non-coder learns from prompting this app
* **You can describe physical movement:** You can tell the AI how you want things to look and feel using normal words like *"make unplaced cards float gently like they are in water"* or *"slide cards apart to make room when dragging."*
* **Tell the AI to keep everything in one file:** If you do not tell the AI to keep everything together, it might try to connect to outside web tools that school filters block. Telling the AI *"keep everything in a single standalone file with no outside links"* ensures it works 100% offline on any school computer.
* **Borrow fun ideas from popular games:** Asking the AI to use familiar rules—like Wordle-style green and yellow feedback—helps create engaging educational games in seconds.
