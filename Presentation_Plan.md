# The Classroom Genie: Building Custom Tools Without Code

**Session:** Pearson Virtual Schools Staff Conference  
**Audience:** Virtual Teachers & Instructional Staff (Zero Coding Experience)  
**Presenter:** Caleob King  
**Core Metaphor:** AI is a **powerful but overly literal Genie**. It can build any educational manipulative you imagine, but because it takes every word literally, you must learn how to state your wishes clearly.

---

## Slide 2: The Three Curses of Ready-Made EdTech
* **Goal:** Name the everyday frustrations virtual teachers face with existing commercial tools.
* **Content:**
  * **The Login Nightmare:** Students forget passwords; apps demand personal accounts and violate student privacy policies.
  * **The Cookie-Cutter Trap:** Off-the-shelf tools never fit your exact lesson, book chapter, or state standard.
  * **The Paywall & Firewall:** Great tools cost money or get blocked by district internet security.
  * **The Genie’s Secret:** Your web browser is already a free, offline projector. It can run self-contained tools saved directly on your desktop—zero logins, zero accounts, zero student data sent to the internet.
* **Visuals:** Split graphic showing a student staring at a red "Access Denied / Login Expired" screen versus a happy teacher double-clicking a file that instantly opens and works offline.

## Slide 3: Meeting the Genie (How Prompting Really Works)
* **Goal:** Establish the "literal genie" mental model with zero technical jargon.
* **Content:**
  * The Genie does not read your mind; it follows your exact words.
  * **Three Rules to Tame the Genie:**
    1. *Keep it in one house:* Tell the Genie to put everything into a single standalone file so nothing gets lost or blocked.
    2. *No phantom files:* Tell the Genie to make all sounds and pictures using your computer's built-in abilities—no downloading mystery image or sound files.
    3. *Protect student privacy:* The app must run only in your browser's temporary memory—no student names or grades stored on cloud servers.
* **Visuals:** A friendly cartoon genie holding a blueprint and asking: "Your wish is my command... but be specific!"

## Slide 4: App 1 — The Spinning Wheel (EduSpin)
* **Goal:** Show how a simple prompt transforms a standard classroom routine into an engaging interactive game.
* **Content:**
  * **Classroom Magic:** Spin for random student turns with sound effects that keep 3rd graders glued to the screen. Switch to dual wheels to pair students or compare two ideas side-by-side.
  * **Safe & Fair:** Automatically removes names after they are picked. Class rosters stay private on your computer.
  * **The Genie Lesson (The Magic Lamp):** Double-clicking an `.html` file opens it right in Chrome or Edge without internet access. You just made your first piece of classroom software!
* **Visuals:** Live demo screenshot of the vibrant dark-mode wheel with a pointer at top, showing "Selected: Maya" and side-by-side compare wheels.

## Slide 5: App 2 — The Elastic Timeline
* **Goal:** Demonstrate hands-on manipulatives that make abstract sequences tangible during screen shares.
* **Content:**
  * **Classroom Magic:** Floating cards bob gently at the bottom of the screen. Drag them onto the timeline bar above, and they smoothly slide apart to make space.
  * **Wordle-Style Feedback:** Click "Check Timeline"—correct cards turn glowing green; misplaced cards glow yellow so students know where the chain broke. Confetti bursts upon a complete victory!
  * **The Genie Lesson (Describing Movement):** You do not need to know programming code. You can describe physical sensations in everyday language: *"make unplaced cards float gently like they are in water"* and *"slide adjacent cards apart to clear space."*
* **Visuals:** Visual capture of the dark slate timeline with cards connected by clean stems, green/amber status glow, and confetti floating down.

## Slide 6: App 3 — Vocabulary Codenames
* **Goal:** Show how teachers can turn any list of words into a collaborative review game with built-in virtual teaching shortcuts.
* **Content:**
  * **Classroom Magic:** Paste any 16 spelling, history, or science terms into a 4x4 card grid. Divides into Red Team, Blue Team, Bystanders, and one surprise trap card.
  * **Virtual Classroom Lifesaver:** Includes one-click copy buttons that format secret team word lists ready to paste directly into Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet private chats for team leaders.
  * **The Genie Lesson (Solving Real Classroom Friction):** Tell the Genie about your actual teaching hurdles. Asking for *"a button to copy secret words for chat"* creates exact workflow shortcuts. No clipart or picture downloads needed!
* **Visuals:** A 4x4 grid of word cards on screen share with teacher peek mode toggled, plus a close-up callout of the "Copy Red Key" chat button.

## Slide 7: App 4 — Rotate & Relate (Geometry Sandbox)
* **Goal:** Illustrate how advanced visual manipulatives unlock tough conceptual hurdles.
* **Content:**
  * **Classroom Magic:** Secondary geometry students struggle to see nested, rotated triangles. This app lets them connect matching sides with virtual red, blue, and yellow cords.
  * **The "Aha!" Moment:** Clicking check triggers a smooth 3-step animation: triangles lift off the screen, flip over, and rotate until they line up side-by-side to visibly prove whether cords match.
  * **The Genie Lesson (The Anatomy of an App):** Every web app has three simple pieces:
    * *The Skeleton* (`.html`): The words and buttons you see.
    * *The Clothes* (`.css`): The colors, fonts, and dark mode.
    * *The Brain* (`.js`): The math, mouse dragging, and animations.
* **Visuals:** An infographic showcasing a windows file explorer showing the three file types.

## Slide 8: App 5 — FeedbackFlow (The Grading Assistant)
* **Goal:** Show how AI helps teachers reclaim their time and iterate on ideas in steps.
* **Content:**
  * **Classroom Magic:** Turn rubric button clicks and quick notes (like "See me in office hours") into a warm, natural feedback paragraph ready to paste into your gradebook.
  * **The Genie Lesson (Wishes in Stages):** You never have to build perfection on the first try:
    * *Wish 1:* "Make a tool where clicking rubric levels builds a feedback paragraph."
    * *Wish 2:* "Can you also let me customize point values (0-8) and show a score summary like `Score: 10/12` at the bottom?"
    * *The Rule:* Tell the Genie what to keep before asking for changes so it doesn't accidentally erase your favorite parts.
* **Visuals:** An infographic showing the flywheel of prompt -> build -> test -> refine and back to prompt again

## Slide 9: The Teacher's Golden Prompt Formula
* **Goal:** Give every attendee a simple, plug-and-play prompt template they can use during their next planning period.
* **Content:**
  * **The 4-Part Wish Formula:**
    1. **Who the Genie is:** *"Act as an educational web tool maker."*
    2. **Who it is for:** *"Build a tool for my 6th-grade Earth Science students."*
    3. **What it does:** *"Students click steps of the water cycle to see an animation."*
    4. **The Genie Guardrails:**
       * *"Keep everything inside one single file that ends in .html."*
       * *"Do not use outside websites, image links, or sound downloads."*
       * *"Keep student information 100% private on my computer."*
* **Visuals:** A clean, colorful card graphic displaying the 4-part formula with large, readable bullet points.

## Slide 10: Step-by-Step Hands-On Guide (No Tech Fear!)
* **Goal:** Eliminate the practical friction of saving and running an HTML file on Windows and Mac.
* **Content:**
  * **Step 1:** Click the floating **"Copy code"** button inside your AI chat window (never try to drag-highlight hundreds of lines of code!).
  * **Step 2:** Open a pre-made empty file (we provide pre-named `tool.html` files for you) in Notepad or TextEdit.
  * **Step 3:** Paste and Save (`Ctrl + S` or `Cmd + S`).
  * **Step 4:** Double-click the file to open it in Chrome, Edge, or Safari. It runs instantly!
* **Visuals:** Three simple picture steps: Copy Button icon ➔ Notepad Paste ➔ Double-click icon in folder.

## Slide 11: Tinker Time (Hands-On Lab & Debrief)
* **Goal:** Give attendees an immediate, low-stakes sandbox to prompt their very first custom tool with live support, followed by a collaborative debrief.
* **Content:**
  * **The Challenge (Prompt Your Wish):** Think of one repetitive classroom chore, review game, or concept your students struggle with. Use the Golden Prompt Formula to ask the Genie for a tool.
  * **The 4 Easy Steps on Screen:**
    1. Fill in the Golden Prompt Shell.
    2. Click the Genie's "Copy code" button.
    3. Paste into your pre-named `tool.html` file and save.
    4. Double-click to test it in your browser!
  * **On-Screen Active Countdown Timer:** A visible 7–10 minute countdown ticking on the main screen so everyone paces their experimentation.
  * **Facilitator Role:** Roam the virtual room/chat to troubleshoot common Genie hiccups (remind teachers to use the "Copy code" button, don't worry about understanding the code, just test the buttons).
  * **Classroom Debrief & Show-and-Tell (Final 3–5 Minutes):**
    * Bring the whole group back together when the timer rings.
    * What did you ask the Genie to make?
    * Did the Genie give you any unexpected surprises or misinterpretations?
    * How does it feel knowing you just created a customized web tool in under 10 minutes?
* **Visuals:** A split slide featuring a prominent, pulsing digital countdown timer on the left, and a quick-reference "Emergency Wish Troubleshooting" card on the right (e.g., *"Did the Genie stop typing? Just type: 'Please keep going from where you stopped.' "*).
