# Example 1: EduSpin Wheel Picker

## What is this app?
This is a fun, colorful digital spinning wheel that runs right inside your internet browser. You can use it as a single wheel to pick one thing at random, or put two wheels side-by-side to pair things up or compare ideas.

## How to use it in a virtual K-12 classroom
* **Random Student Caller:** Paste your student roster into the wheel. When it is time to answer questions or read aloud, give it a spin. The exciting tick sounds and visual spin keep every student watching their screen.
* **Fair Turns:** Turn on the setting to remove names after they are picked so nobody gets picked twice in a row.
* **Side-by-Side Compare & Contrast:** Put historical figures on Wheel 1 and scientific events on Wheel 2, or characters on one and story conflicts on the other. Spin both and have students explain how the two items connect.
* **100% Student Privacy:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or upload student names to the cloud. Your class roster stays safely on your own computer.

## What a non-coder learns from prompting this app
* **Your browser is a free player:** You do not need expensive software subscriptions to run classroom tools. A simple file ending in `.html` can open right in Chrome or Edge and work instantly.
* **No missing parts:** When asking AI for sound or pictures, you can tell it to create sounds using the computer's built-in sound card and draw shapes with simple math. That way, you never have to download extra sound or image files that might get lost or blocked by school firewalls.
* **You can change how it works:** If you want two wheels instead of one, you simply ask the AI in plain English: "Add an option for two wheels side-by-side."
