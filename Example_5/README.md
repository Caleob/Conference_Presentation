# Example 5: Rotate & Relate (Geometry Triangle Sandbox)

## What is this app?
This is a hands-on geometry manipulative for teaching similar right triangles. In math class, when an altitude line cuts through a big right triangle, it creates smaller triangles that are turned upside down and backward. This app lets students connect matching sides using virtual colored cords, type out math similarity statements, and press a button to watch the triangles magically lift off the page, flip over, and rotate until they line up side-by-side.

## How to use it in a virtual K-12 classroom
* **Make Invisible Math Visible:** Instead of struggling to see how overlapping shapes connect on a flat textbook page, students see live shapes on your shared screen.
* **Hands-on Virtual Wires:** Students or the teacher can drag red, blue, and yellow wires between corresponding short sides, long sides, and diagonal sides to test their thinking.
* **The "Aha!" Moment Animation:** When students click to check their answer, the app animates the transformation—gliding, flipping, and rotating the triangles together so students instantly see whether their colored lines match up or clash.
* **Unlimited Fresh Practice:** Click "New Triangle" to get a brand-new randomized shape with different angles and letters for endless practice problems.

## What a non-coder learns from prompting this app
* **Clear plans lead to amazing apps:** When asking AI for something complex, giving it a clear blueprint (like explaining step-by-step: "Draw the shape, give me 3 colored wires, and animate the rotation") produces results that look like commercial software.
* **How web pages are built (The 3 Pieces):** This example shows how web pages work behind the scenes:
  1. *The Skeleton* (`Elements_On_Screen.html`): The words, buttons, and shapes you see.
  2. *The Clothes* (`How_It_Looks.css`): The colors, fonts, and dark theme.
  3. *The Brain* (`What_It_Does.js`): The math, mouse dragging, and smooth animations.
* **You are in full control:** You do not need to settle for clunky, locked-down textbook websites. With a simple, clear description, AI can craft an interactive manipulative tailored to the exact topic your students are struggling with.
